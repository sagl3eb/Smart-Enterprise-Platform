II.

A small manifesto
for running
a company quietly.

Five convictions we kept refusing to compromise.

I.

Software should be legible.

Every screen is a sentence. If a screen cannot be read in five seconds by someone who has never seen it before, it is not yet finished. We finish screens.

II.

The dashboard is not the product.

Dashboards are the preface. The product is the decision you made because of what you saw. SEP is optimized for the decision.

III.

Predictions owe explanations.

An ML model that cannot say why it flagged an employee does not belong in a review. Every prediction is accompanied by its three strongest contributing factors.

IV.

Boredom is a feature.

The platform should be the least exciting part of Monday. Automation is what quiet looks like from the outside. A quiet Monday means the system is working.

V.

The smallest customer gets the same software as the largest.

No segmented product for the SME. No "enterprise tier" locking the useful things. The same instrument, priced to the size of the company using it — because organizations grow in both directions.
